## (e) Key Asymmetric Findings

### 1. Mars: The Decisive Asymmetry (Ruchaka vs Debilitated)

The single largest structural gap between the two charts:

- **Ankur:** Mars is **exalted** in Capricorn (10 deg), placed in the 7th House of the D-9 Navamsa. Since the Navamsa Lagna is Cancer (4), the 7th house is a kendra — this is a textbook **Ruchaka Mahapurusha Yoga** (disciplined, strategic will — the warrior archetype in its highest form). Mars functions as **Yogakaraka** for Cancer Lagna (lord of H2+H7), making its exaltation doubly potent — it simultaneously governs wealth accumulation and partnership dharma.
- **Modi:** Mars is **debilitated** in Cancer (according to standard Parashari dignity). His Mars operates from weakness, requiring external yoga formations (NBRY) to function.

This asymmetry was identified as the critical differentiator in the D-9 comparison and drives the 29-point gap.

The initial AI scoring had **overlooked** the Sun-Mercury Maha Parivartana Raja Yoga (4th Lord Sun - 2nd+5th Lord Mercury). This was surfaced during the adversarial sparring session when the user challenged "if you gonna give me a handicap, im gonna lose."

**Effect of the correction:**

- Flipped the D-1 scorecard in Ankur's favor: from a presumed deficit to +7 margin (61 vs 54)

### 3. Saturn Raja Yogas: Disaggregated

The AI had **collapsed** Saturn's aspects into a single line item. The user disaggregated them into individual Raja Yoga pairs:
- **Saturn (9L+10L) aspecting Sun (4L)** — Dharma-Karmadhipati Raja Yoga: religious fortune (9th) + career (10th) aspecting home/foundation (4th)
- **Saturn (9L+10L) aspecting Mars (3L+8L)** — additional Raja Yoga: courage/initiative (3rd) + transformation (8th) linked to dharma and career

Saturn's internal **9th+10th lordship union** itself constitutes a **Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga** — the native's religious/spiritual path and professional career are the same axis.

### 4. Guru-Mangala Yoga

Jupiter (9th Lord for Taurus Lagna) + Mars (3rd+8th Lord) in the 5th House (Virgo) — this is a named classical Raja Yoga representing the convergence of wisdom/dharma (Jupiter) with disciplined action/warrior energy (Mars) in the house of creativity, intellect, and purva punya.

### 5. Lordship Quality Score (10/10)

The only perfect score in the comparison. Every planet in Ankur's chart eventually disposits back into the exchange axis (Sun-Mercury) or into the Yogakaraka Saturn. There is no "leakage" — no planet that governs a dusthana and deposits into another dusthana without passing through a functional benefic first.

### 6. Modi's Counter-Architecture

Modi's strength is in **persona-level projection**:
- Ruchaka Yoga (Mars dignity in his chart) — gives personal magnetism and warrior presence
